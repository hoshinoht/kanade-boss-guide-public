Overview

Annihilation gauge · Extreme

  • Phase 1 passive▼+2%/s, against you
  • Phase 2 passive▼+2.7%/s, against you
  • Large arm hits Lotus▲≈ −16 to −22%, in your favour
  • Full in Phases 1-2▼34 s Annihilation, against you
  • Empty in Phase 3▲25 s Safe Mode, in your favour

Phase 3 has no passive rise.

Bombardment safe spots

  • Top-leftPhases 2-3 · the default
  • RightPhase 1
  • Bottom-rightPhases 2-3 · knockback risk

In Phase 3, portals link the two safe corners: use them to switch sides when the bombardment comes.

Core

  • Annihilation gaugeOne per player; rises on its own in Phases 1-2 and from hits. Full means Annihilation Mode's extra patterns.
  • Lasers and armsHitting you raises your gauge; hitting Lotus lowers it. Extreme adds a large arm after every 10th small one.
  • Per playerThe gauge freezes while you are dead; in a duo, Annihilation patterns are per player.

Danger

  • Horizontal bombardmentLethal beam; see Bombardment safe spots. The firing cannon itself is safe.
  • Large arm~1 s warning on Extreme; 70% HP in Phase 1, 80% in Phases 2-3.
  • Darkness AuraPhase 3: stand on Lotus to dodge it, unless orange Gravity Restraint lasers bind you; then it deals 100%.
  • Drill Push and Current DischargeDrill Push into a corner hits twice. Current Discharge: 100% and a 2 s stun unless you leave the floor.
  • Bottom-right knockbackExtreme Phase 3 bottom-right can knock you into the beam.

Tips

  • Clear the shieldExtreme: a full arm set plus the large arm clears it; drag them through Lotus.
  • Drill PushDuck its rear spawn; never take it in a corner.
  • Ring swapOz-ring swappers can swap under the shield; damage is cut 90% anyway.

Phases

Phase 1

white shield

  • White shield Every ~40 s: −90% damage for 20 s. Leftover shield heals Lotus, up to 20% max HP.
  • Strip it Aim lasers and arms through Lotus; binding stops only the shield pattern.
  • Clear the shield Extreme: one full arm set plus the large arm clears it; stand where you can drag them through Lotus.
  • Bombardment Safe on the right in Phase 1; the firing cannon itself is safe.
  • Large arm About 1 s warning on Extreme; hits 70% HP in Phase 1.

Phase 3

reversed gauge

  • Reversed gauge Annihilation Mode is the default. Only map attacks on Lotus lower the gauge; empty it for Safe Mode.
  • Drain sources Aim all three tracking lasers, the marked arms (above all the large arm) and missiles through Lotus.
  • Safe Mode burst Burst in the 25 s Safe Mode, then drain again as soon as Annihilation resumes.
  • Darkness Aura Stand on Lotus to dodge it, unless orange Gravity Restraint lasers bind you; then it deals 100%.
  • Bombardment corners Top-left is the default; portals link the safe corners. On Extreme, bottom-right can knock you into the beam.

Strategies

Options, not orders: each trades risk against the damage it needs.
