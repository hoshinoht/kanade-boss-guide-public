Jupiter

Keep the shared needle centred (401–600) at every Command and merge; drift stacks Rupture and brings the harsher merged forms.

  • Rupture Personal; 1000 ejects you. Deaths add 150 separated, 200 merged.
  • Bond Initiative Up a stage per Auspicious or Ruinous merge, down one per Balanced. Stages 2–3 tax every hit.
  • Phase thresholds One HP bar: Phase 2 at 80%, Phase 3 at 50%. Separated time 150 → 120 → 90s.

Danger

  • Phase 3 Command shove Each Command throws the needle to 350/650, or 150 further out; re-centre within 30s.
  • Lance Protocol Jupiter-side lasers swing the gauge hardest.
  • Branding combo Phase 3 Entity, and Ruinous from Phase 1: two brands make the closer deal 200% HP.
  • Tracking blasts Phase 3 Auspicious (Colossus Impact) and Ruinous (Illusion Kill): 200% HP over a wide radius.
  • Auspicious marks All must reach their mark twice or everyone gains 50 Rupture. Spectral Bane cuts damage 80%.
  • Ruinous Fastest, hardest form; cannon bounces and portals can chain into a death.

Tips

  • One needle caller Correct about 15s before each Command (20s from the outer band); ask the other side to balance.
  • Correct with chosen hits At full HP: Arcane Rift or Ether Racer push down, Tracking Instinct pushes up.
  • Phase 1 matters most Failed balance costs most there; one bad merge is recoverable.
  • Warp and binds Warp when one side is overloaded; debuffs persist. Plan binds before merges.
  • Crouch and step Crouch Double Sided Attack, Tackle, Cruel Claw, Execution A; step between Mana Plum pillars.
  • Hard forced merges Save i-frames and Ascent skills for them.

Phases

Alternating states · repeats

Separated two sides · 3 Commands
  • Two sides Jupiter left, Entity right, max two each. Jupiter hits push the needle down, Entity hits push it up.
  • Ether Warp Swaps sides (NPC/Gather key); cooldown 15/20/30s solo/duo/trio.
  • Commands Three per state, every 50/40/30s by phase. Centred: −10 Rupture. Off-centre: +60 to +100.
  • Drift 5s outside the blue band, the needle slides out 25 per 3s; you take up to 10/15% more damage.
  • Phase 3 shove Each Command throws the needle to 350/650, or 150 further out; re-centre within 30s.
Merged (100s) three forms
  • Three forms Set by the needle after the third Command: Balanced, Auspicious (0–400) or Ruinous (601–1000).
  • Balanced Own −7..+7 needle: end within one of 0 for −40 Rupture; far out costs up to +60.
  • Auspicious and Ruinous No needle; just survive.
  • Auspicious marks Everyone must reach their mark twice or all gain 50 Rupture; Spectral Bane zones cut damage 80%.
  • Ruinous Fastest and hardest; cannon bounces and portals chain deaths, and it runs the two-brand combo from Phase 1.

Strategies

One left, two right

Medium risk · Medium damage requirement

Trios with one strong, steady-uptime player (iSIingGunz guide).

  1. In the waiting room one player takes Jupiter's side (left) alone; the other two take the Alchemical Entity (right), whose movement suits burst classes.
  2. Putting the strongest player left is a party-building habit, not a rule; whoever goes left must also steer the needle reliably.

Payoff Steadiest needle, because Jupiter-side lasers swing it hardest.

Two left, swap in P3

Medium risk · Medium damage requirement

Trios still learning the fight, and solo or duo runs (Korean Inven guide).

  1. Solo and duo players fight on Jupiter's side; a duo may burst together there and split otherwise.
  2. In a trio, Phases 1-2 put two on Jupiter and one on the Entity.
  3. From Phase 3 swap to one on Jupiter and two on the Entity, since the Entity side starts branding and Commands come every 30s. Say who swaps in the run briefing.

Payoff Less damage lost while the Entity sinks or teleports out of reach (it is invincible then).

Notes

  • Limits 20 min, 10s potions; no slows or critical binds.
  • Shared HP and binds The five bodies share one bar; bind resistance carries between rooms.
  • HP estimates Overdrive HP is estimated from the published KMS reductions.
