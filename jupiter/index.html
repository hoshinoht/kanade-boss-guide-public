Overview

Magic Synchronization

  1. 0–400 Auspicious
  2. 401–600 centred
  3. 601–1000 Ruinous

Shared needle. Jupiter-side hits push it down, Entity-side hits push it up.

Rupture · personal, out at 1000

  • Death (separated / merged)▼+150 / +200, against you
  • Command centred▲−10, in your favour
  • Command off-centre▼+60 / +80 / +100, against you
  • Balanced merge within 1 of 0▲−40, in your favour
  • Balanced merge far out▼up to +60, against you
  • Hit at Bond stage 2 / 3▼+5 / +10, against you
  • Reaching Bond stage 3▼+100, against you
  • Auspicious marks (all reached / a miss)−10 / +50 each

Core

  • RupturePersonal; 1000 ejects you. Deaths add 150 separated, 200 merged.
  • Bond InitiativeUp a stage per Auspicious or Ruinous merge, down one per Balanced. Stages 2–3 tax every hit.
  • Phase thresholdsOne HP bar: Phase 2 at 80%, Phase 3 at 50%. Separated time 150 → 120 → 90s.

Danger

  • Phase 3 Command shoveEach Command throws the needle to 350/650, or 150 further out; re-centre within 30s.
  • Lance ProtocolJupiter-side lasers swing the gauge hardest.
  • Branding comboPhase 3 Entity, and Ruinous from Phase 1: two brands make the closer deal 200% HP.
  • Tracking blastsPhase 3 Auspicious (Colossus Impact) and Ruinous (Illusion Kill): 200% HP over a wide radius.
  • Auspicious marksAll must reach their mark twice or everyone gains 50 Rupture. Spectral Bane cuts damage 80%.
  • RuinousFastest, hardest form; cannon bounces and portals can chain into a death.

Tips

  • One needle callerCorrect about 15s before each Command (20s from the outer band); ask the other side to balance.
  • Correct with chosen hitsAt full HP: Arcane Rift or Ether Racer push down, Tracking Instinct pushes up.
  • Phase 1 matters mostFailed balance costs most there; one bad merge is recoverable.
  • Warp and bindsWarp when one side is overloaded; debuffs persist. Plan binds before merges.
  • Crouch and stepCrouch Double Sided Attack, Tackle, Cruel Claw, Execution A; step between Mana Plum pillars.
  • Hard forced mergesSave i-frames and Ascent skills for them.

Phases

↻Alternating states · repeats

↻Alternating states · repeats

Separated

two sides · 3 Commands

  • Two sides Jupiter left, Entity right, max two each. Jupiter hits push the needle down, Entity hits push it up.
  • Ether Warp Swaps sides (NPC/Gather key); cooldown 15/20/30s solo/duo/trio.
  • Commands Three per state, every 50/40/30s by phase. Centred: −10 Rupture. Off-centre: +60 to +100.
  • Drift 5s outside the blue band, the needle slides out 25 per 3s; you take up to 10/15% more damage.
  • Phase 3 shove Each Command throws the needle to 350/650, or 150 further out; re-centre within 30s.

↻Alternating states · repeats

Merged (100s)

three forms

  • Three forms Set by the needle after the third Command: Balanced, Auspicious (0–400) or Ruinous (601–1000).
  • Balanced Own −7..+7 needle: end within one of 0 for −40 Rupture; far out costs up to +60.
  • Auspicious and Ruinous No needle; just survive.
  • Auspicious marks Everyone must reach their mark twice or all gain 50 Rupture; Spectral Bane zones cut damage 80%.
  • Ruinous Fastest and hardest; cannon bounces and portals chain deaths, and it runs the two-brand combo from Phase 1.

Strategies

Options, not orders: each trades risk against the damage it needs.

  • One left, two right

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    Medium
    When
    Trios with one strong, steady-uptime player (iSIingGunz guide).
    Payoff
    Steadiest needle, because Jupiter-side lasers swing it hardest.
    1. 1In the waiting room one player takes Jupiter's side (left) alone; the other two take the Alchemical Entity (right), whose movement suits burst classes.
    2. 2Putting the strongest player left is a party-building habit, not a rule; whoever goes left must also steer the needle reliably.

  • Two left, swap in P3

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    Medium
    When
    Trios still learning the fight, and solo or duo runs (Korean Inven guide).
    Payoff
    Less damage lost while the Entity sinks or teleports out of reach (it is invincible then).
    1. 1Solo and duo players fight on Jupiter's side; a duo may burst together there and split otherwise.
    2. 2In a trio, Phases 1-2 put two on Jupiter and one on the Entity.
    3. 3From Phase 3 swap to one on Jupiter and two on the Entity, since the Entity side starts branding and Commands come every 30s. Say who swaps in the run briefing.

Notes

  • Limits20 min, 10s potions; no slows or critical binds.
  • Shared HP and bindsThe five bodies share one bar; bind resistance carries between rooms.
  • HP estimatesOverdrive HP is estimated from the published KMS reductions.
