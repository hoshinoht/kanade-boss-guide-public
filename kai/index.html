Overview

Chrono Gauge · max 1000

  • Chrono Break off▲+24 per second, in your favour
  • Start Chrono Break▼−90, against you
  • Keep it on (after 3s)▼−30 per second, against you
  • Getting hit▼drops, against you
  • Dodge during Chrono Break▲+1 Step charge, in your favour

Fever Count at the end of Distortion

  • 6,000▲−10s cooldowns, in your favour
  • 8,000▲−20s cooldowns, in your favour
  • 10,000▲−40s CDs + 10s groggy, in your favour

Core

  • RulesSolo, 20 minutes, 5 lives. One HP bar; at 50% damage pauses while he becomes Phase 2.
  • Weekly clearOne per Maple ID (reset Wednesday 23:59 SGT), outside the weekly boss limit.
  • Chrono GaugeMax 1000; refills while Chrono Break is off, drops when you are hit.
  • Time DistortionWhen the side timer ends (about 20s, 33s with Chrono Break). Too fast to dodge without Chrono Break (Tab).
  • Chrono StepSpace: a dodge like FA's parry, no damage reward. 3 charges, 5s recharge; hold down to dodge in place.
  • Fever CountRises in Time Distortion, drops on hits; the end total cuts cooldowns.
  • No binds in DistortionNor in groggy (Origin absolute binds excepted). Reaching 50% mid-Distortion ends it early.

Danger

  • Holographic AfterimagePhase 1's hardest: three sets of four strikes, then a late final strike.
  • Distortion lasersCost the most Fever; on Hard one hit can rule out 10,000.
  • High Speed SlashPhase 2: about 126 slashes; Step refunds cap at five.
  • Edge laser wallsGreen walls guard both edges; do not camp corners.
  • Hard extrasSwiftness teleports in and stuns 4s; map patterns drain Chrono Gauge.

Tips

  • Sound onStep to the rhythm of Holographic Afterimage.
  • Sweeping lasersStand mid-map; Step toward the incoming laser.
  • Dragon ThunderStep in place on a warning, or Step into it as it fires.
  • Great WhirlwindStep near the blue safe zones for refunds on later hits.
  • Stand on himAvoids the Phase 2 Recall Strike hologram and sword waves.
  • Burst around groggyA 10,000 Fever run gives about 1.5x the bursts.

Notes

  • Season overNot in the boss catalog; the MapleSEA season ended on 30 Sep 2026. Kept in case Kai returns.

Recommended · Normal Solo in the low-to-mid 20k range (with Sol Hecate).
