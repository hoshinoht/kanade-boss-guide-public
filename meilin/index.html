Overview

Remnants of Destiny · 0–1,000

  • Over time▲slow fill, in your favour
  • Destiny pattern cleared▲large fill, in your favour
  • Her hits▼drains, against you
  • Destiny pattern failed▼drains, against you
  • Diagonal explosions (Hard)▼drains, against you

Full gauge: altar mid-map; use it quickly or the gauge resets.

Core

  • RulesChallengers World characters only. Solo, 20 minutes, 5 lives, 10s potions, 380% PDR.
  • Weekly clearOne per Maple ID across Normal and Hard (KMS reset Thursday 00:00), outside the boss limit.
  • Remnants of Destiny0–1,000 gauge, filled mostly by clearing Destiny patterns (far less on Hard).
  • Altar and ChaosA full gauge spawns the altar: take one of three other-class bursts and fire it once.
  • OrderChaos from all three icon classes earns one random Tab skill.
  • Destiny patternsEvery 55s; she is defenceless but bind-immune. Clear the mechanic, then hit her.
  • Stay glued to herBlind spots are on her; about 700px away or 300px up and she teleports onto you.

Danger

  • Cards of Truth and Lies7 cards shuffle 4 times (6 on Hard); every false card becomes a blade: 80% HP, death on Hard.
  • Swallowing StormPhase 2: a gust shoves you, then blades fill the map. Only the golden barrier is safe.
  • Soul SwordsPhase 2, her worst: three swords flash, then slam in order. Sweeps reach behind; any hit fails.
  • Hand of FatePhase 2: a circle tracks you about 3s, then a hand stabs (50%/55%) with stun or launch.
  • Teleport blastStray or jump too high and she blinks to you and blasts. The blink is harmless; move off.
  • Wave attackEye open: face her. Eye closed: face away. Wrong costs 30%/40% and a stun.

Tips

  • Stay on top of herBlades, charge and pushes are easier point-blank. Charge: dark blue on top, jump; below, crouch.
  • Best Chaos picksEvan Zodiac Ray, Hoyoung Mountain Spirit Summon, then buffs. Skip Night Lord Spread Throw.
  • Spend Order on a burstA 2-minute or Origin burst. Bind Order stacks with your binds; +1 Ascent is the weakest roll.

Phases

Phase 1

four Destiny patterns

  • Four Destiny patterns Area of Rest, Flame and Freeze swords, Arrows of Destiny, Cards of Truth and Lies.
  • Area of Rest No movement skills. Walk the top platform; touch each blue section as its ring closes.
  • Flame and Freeze Ignore her swing; stand in the slanted zone matching your head icon until it hits 0.
  • Arrows of Destiny Drag each arrow through a purple spirit; pull them through the middle near her.
  • Cards of Truth and Lies 7 cards shuffle 4 times (6 on Hard); every false card becomes a blade: 80% HP, death on Hard.

Phase 2 (from 50%)

two more patterns

  • Transformation At 50% she turns immune, strips all debuffs and binds, and transforms.
  • Two more patterns Swallowing Storm and Soul Swords join the rotation.
  • Doubled attacks Wave attack and teleport blast each fire twice.
  • Soul Swords Her worst: three swords flash, then slam in order. Sweeps reach behind; any hit fails.
  • Swallowing Storm A gust shoves you, then blades fill the map; only the golden barrier is safe.

Strategies

Options, not orders: each trades risk against the damage it needs.

  • Use Chaos and Order

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    Medium
    When
    Standard approach at or near the recommended spec; first clears
    Payoff
    About one extra borrowed burst per minute plus an Order skill roughly every three minutes
    1. 1Stay glued to Meilin and clear every Destiny pattern so the gauge fills about once per pattern cycle.
    2. 2Take the altar pick at once, choosing picks that complete the three class icons for an Order.
    3. 3Fire each held Chaos skill in a burst or semi-burst before the next full gauge, so the altar does not overwrite it.

  • P2 sword and storm iframes

    Risk
    Low
    Damage need
    Medium
    When
    Classes with Genesis liberation invincibility, Ascent, or short i-frames (e.g. Shadower, Mihile)
    Payoff
    Fewer deaths and missed gauge on the two hardest Destiny patterns, with free damage during them
    1. 1Swallowing Storm, stand inside the golden barrier and use Ascent or a short i-frame through the shove instead of riding the wind.
    2. 2Soul Swords, start Genesis invincibility around the second sword and cover the last sword with Ascent; on Normal a long invincibility can cover the whole pattern.
    3. 3Do not use invincibility on Flame and Freeze; an invincible clear there gives no gauge.

  • Overgear: focus on damage

    Risk
    Low
    Damage need
    High
    When
    Well above the recommended spec
    Payoff
    Simplest run; Chaos and Order are only bonus damage, not a clear requirement
    1. 1Dodge Destiny patterns safely rather than chasing gauge, and use Chaos or Order only when they come up.

  • Retry for better Order

    Risk
    High
    Damage need
    Low
    When
    Below the recommended spec, aiming for Hard at the lowest possible stats
    Payoff
    Hard clear under spec, but it needs many retries because Order rolls are random
    1. 1Clear every Destiny pattern so no gauge is lost, and spend every burst and semi-burst on cooldown (one guide counts 11 windows).
    2. 2Restart runs with poor Order rolls; one guide ranks reset-5th, then +20% final damage, then the bind, though the best roll depends on class.
    3. 3Hide heavy skill and Soul effects so the diagonal explosions and gauge stay readable.
