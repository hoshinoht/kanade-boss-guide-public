Overview

Four weapons

  • Eye of Restraintleft · stun laser
  • Bombardment Fightertop · drone beam
  • Odium spherebouncing ball
  • Eye of the Abyssright · dark arrows

Shoot the Eyes directly; hit the drone and ball through the generator above or below them as they pass.

Weapon activation

  • Easyevery 80 s
  • Normalevery 70 s
  • Chaos and Extremeevery 60 s
  • Watcher's Roar▼+1 weapon, against you

MapleSEA v251 slowed Easy and Normal from 60 s.

Shots per party member

  • Each Eye2 · Chaos/Extreme 3
  • Drone1 · Chaos/Extreme 2
  • BallEasy 1 · all others 2

A weapon's colour counts its shots left: red, then purple, then dark blue.

Phase 2 segments

  1. 2-1 · 100–75%
  2. 2-2 · 75–50%
  3. 2-3 · 50–25%
  4. 2-4 · curses

Each segment ends in a dome test; segment 2-4 adds a curse every minute.

Core

  • Four weaponsEye of Restraint (left, stun laser), Eye of the Abyss (right, dark arrows), Bombardment Fighter (top drone) and the bouncing Odium sphere.
  • ActivationOne more switches on per timer (set by difficulty) and per Watcher's Roar.
  • Will of KalosNPC/Gather key to aim, again to fire; refills about every 10 s.
  • Shots neededScale with party size and difficulty.
  • All four onAll four still on when the next timer ends ejects the party; freeing any one resets it.

Danger

  • Eye of RestraintStun laser that chains into his attacks, even in dome tests. Freeing it cancels the shot (SEA v251).
  • Empowered weaponsChaos/Extreme: three or more active empower all; empowered arrows kill in two hits.
  • Watcher's RoarOne-shot every ~2.5 min that adds a weapon; binds delay it by their length.
  • Breath and divePhase 2 Laser Breath kills through Heaven's Door, one floor up too; the dive picks a random player.
  • Curses below 25%Segment 2-4: a curse every minute; two stacks raise damage taken, a third kills. Harvest the armguard to clear.

Tips

  • Caller and timerOne watches Kalos after each teleport; one times Roar, breath, dive and next system.
  • Number the generatorsCall 1-5 for drone and ball shots so nobody doubles up.
  • Ball timingFire when it is halfway to its generator; the barrier lasts about 2 s.
  • Hero's WillUse it before the left tower's laser lands.
  • Authentic SymbolHotel Arcs Lv 11: +20% damage to Kalos (SEA v251).

Phases

Phase 2

dome test every 25%

  • Dome tests Each 25% segment ends in a three-dome test. A death heals Kalos 5%; success gives a 15 s groggy, timers paused.
  • Left tower in tests Eye of Restraint keeps firing during dome tests; never start one while it is active.
  • Threshold control Stop just above each 25% mark (parties use about 0.3-1%); phase once Left is off and fewer than three weapons are up.
  • Curses in 2-4 Below 25% a curse lands every minute: two stacks raise damage taken, a third kills. Harvest the armguard to clear.
  • Breath and dive Laser Breath kills through Heaven's Door and reaches one floor up; the dive lands on a random player.

Strategies

Options, not orders: each trades risk against the damage it needs.

  • Clear left first

    Risk
    Low
    Damage need
    Low
    When
    Easy and Normal, learning parties or low shot accuracy
    Payoff
    Fewest stun-chain deaths and safe dome tests
    1. 1Cleanse Eye of Restraint first, then usually Eye of the Abyss; whether the ball or the drone comes next is the caller's choice.
    2. 2Shoot from an angle you have practised and check that each shot connects.
    3. 3Never start a dome test while the Left tower is active.
    4. 4Hold a charged shot when the next system is about 10 s away, instead of firing at nothing.

  • Prepare weapons

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    High
    When
    Chaos and Extreme, where three active weapons empower all of them; an advanced party convention that needs a caller
    Payoff
    Keeps the active count under three at tests and bursts
    1. 1Shoot one weapon (often the ball or drone) down to its last colour and leave it there as a reserve, with one named player owning it.
    2. 2When a third weapon switches on, the owner calls and finishes the reserve at once to drop back to two; never sit on three empowered weapons.
    3. 3Leave a nearly finished weapon up until the next activation, so you do not re-roll a worse one.
    4. 4Some routes put the Right tower ahead of Left while arrows are empowered; agree that exception in advance, and keep shooting during tests and bursts.

  • Hold HP, then burst

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    High
    When
    Any difficulty; the main party damage plan, with an entry sequence and stop margin each party calibrates
    Payoff
    One long burst per segment and fewer weapon cycles
    1. 1Phase 1 - group on one side, wait for his first teleport so everyone has loaded, then bind and burst.
    2. 2Phase 2 start - group in the middle so his opening dive lands there, step out, then bind and burst.
    3. 3Stop just above each 25% threshold (about 0.3-1% is a party habit, not an official margin; allow for lingering damage) and phase once Left is off and fewer than three weapons are up, before the next Roar adds a weapon.
    4. 4Burst again in the 15 s groggy after each dome test, and burst through segment 2-4 quickly.
