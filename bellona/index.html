Overview

Two states

  • Normalregular patterns
  • Insanered only · she takes +30%

About 30s each with no hits.

Gauge and lives (solo values)

  • Red hit (Easy/Normal/Hard)▼−200/400/600, against you
  • Purple hit (Easy/Normal/Hard)▼−80/80/160, against you
  • Any red hit · lives▼−1 Death Count, against you
  • Clean Insane window · lives▲+1 Death Count, in your favour

Core

  • Red attacks cost a lifeRed hits take 1 Death Count and cut the gauge hard; purple hits cut it a little.
  • Shared gauge0–1000, party-wide, moving about 31 per second: roughly 30s per state.
  • Insane modeRed attacks only; each one hurts her, and she takes +30% damage.
  • Clean Insane, +1 lifeSurvive a whole window un-hit to regain a life (that player only). Any hit heals her.
  • Sigils start BerserkThree sigils trigger a three-weapon attack chain.
  • Binds skip a stateOutside her rampage, a bind flips to the next state.
  • Hidden markersNormal and Hard hide most ranges; Hard is faster.

Danger

  • Double-axe BerserkHands fill the map (Phase 1 under 50%, all of Phase 2). Bind it or use invincibility.
  • Phase 2 below 50%Pinball adds a final slam (up-jump); the border sequence adds a bottom-centre charge.
  • Hits snowballOne hit invites panicked follow-ups; failed runs lose lives in a row.

Tips

  • Vanish? Crouch firstUpright somersault: crouch. Inverted: jump. In Insane, wait for the full red form.
  • Horizontal, vertical, XCrouch, dash through on time (never early), stand still. Call Phase 2's triple tell aloud.
  • Bottom-centre chargeUp-jump. Axe raised: dash behind her. Axe to the side: stay crouched.
  • PinballDefault to the left side.
  • Read sigils in bindsThey show the next Berserk sequence.

Strategies

Options, not orders: each trades risk against the damage it needs.

  • Dodge first

    Risk
    Low
    Damage need
    High
    When
    First clears or below the minimum cut
    Payoff
    Clean Insane windows come about once a minute and refund lives; slower clear
    1. 1Stop attacking during every tell; add damage only once dodges are consistent.
    2. 2Treat each Insane window as a no-hit check; a clean one refunds a life.
    3. 3After a hit, reset and focus on the next tell instead of chasing damage.

  • Share spare lives

    Risk
    Medium
    Damage need
    Medium
    When
    Duo or trio where one member has spare Death Count
    Payoff
    Teammates may refund lives more often (unconfirmed interpretation of the source)
    1. 1The gauge is shared but the Insane refund is personal; the member with spare lives may take a pattern on purpose so others can finish Insane clean.
    2. 2Hits during Insane heal Bellona, so only do this when a teammate is short on lives.
